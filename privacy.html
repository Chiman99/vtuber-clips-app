vtuber-clips privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

What vtuber-clips accesses

When you sign in with Google, vtuber-clips asks for access to your YouTube account (videos, playlists, comments) and read-only access to your YouTube Analytics. It uses this access only to carry out actions the channel owner requests: uploading videos and thumbnails, reading channel and video data, editing video details and playlists, replying to comments, and reading analytics.

Where data is stored

The OAuth token and any data fetched from YouTube stay on the channel owner's own computer. vtuber-clips has no server and does not upload this data anywhere else.

Sharing

vtuber-clips does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

vtuber-clips's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke vtuber-clips's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also ends access from that computer.

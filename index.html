vtuber-clips

vtuber-clips is a personal tool for managing one YouTube channel that the developer owns. It uploads videos and thumbnails, reads channel and video data, edits video details and playlists, replies to comments, and reads channel analytics through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API.

It is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in to it.
